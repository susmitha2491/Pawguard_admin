# Volunteer Management

## Purpose
The Volunteer Management module organizes community volunteer involvement. It handles volunteer registrations, skill cataloging, safety orientation, shift scheduling, task assignments, and service hour tracking across shelter operations, rescue support, and public events.

## Roles Involved
- Super Administrator
- Volunteer Coordinator
- Shelter Manager

## Workflow
1. Interested individuals submit volunteer applications via public portals.
2. The Volunteer Coordinator reviews applications, verifies background information, and conducts orientation.
3. Volunteer profiles are updated with verified skills, interest areas, and availability schedules.
4. Shift rosters and task assignments are created for shelter duties, public awareness events, or administrative support.
5. Volunteers complete assigned shifts, and shift attendance and service hours are recorded.
6. The system tracks volunteer service milestones and generates participation certificates.

## Main Operations
- Register, screen, and maintain volunteer records, skills, and emergency contact info.
- Create and publish volunteer shift schedules, event task rosters, and duty assignments.
- Log volunteer attendance, completed service hours, and task fulfillment.
- Issue service certificates, appreciation badges, and training documentation.
- Export volunteer program analytics, active roster counts, and total service hours.

## Status / Lifecycle
A volunteer record moves through:

Application → Orientation & Approved → Active Roster → Shift Assigned → Service Logged

- **Application**: New volunteer registration submitted.
- **Orientation & Approved**: Background checked and safety orientation completed.
- **Active Roster**: Available for shift scheduling.
- **Shift Assigned**: Scheduled for specific shelter or event duties.
- **Service Logged**: Shift completed and service hours added to profile.

## Role Handoffs
- **Volunteer Coordinator → Shelter Manager / Event Leads**: Volunteers assigned to support facility operations or public awareness events.

## Related Process
Connects with **Shelter Management** for facility duty rosters and **Reports & Analytics** for volunteer hour tracking.
