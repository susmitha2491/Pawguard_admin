## 1. About the Super Admin Role

The **Super Administrator** (`super_admin`) is the highest-level administrative role in the PAWGUARD Admin Portal.

The Super Admin has global system-wide access across all platform modules, organizational settings, role and permission management (RBAC), user accounts, audit trails, and administrative configuration.

### Primary Responsibilities

- **Global Platform Administration**: Manage organization-wide settings, system preferences, and platform configurations.
- **User & Account Management**: Register, edit, assign roles, and manage user accounts (`/users`).
- **RBAC & Permissions**: Control role definitions, permissions catalog, and role assignments.
- **Audit Logs & Security**: Inspect system audit logs, access logs, and security events.
- **Operational Oversight**: Full administrative access across Rescue Management, Dispatch Management, Dog Master Profiles, Shelter Management, Vehicle Fleet, Medical Records, Adoptions, Fosters, Volunteers, Inventory, Finance, and Reports.
