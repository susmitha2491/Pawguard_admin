# Super Administrator

## Role Overview
The Super Administrator is the central administrative authority within the PawGuard platform. This role possesses complete operational and administrative visibility across all organization modules, user accounts, system preferences, security governance, and analytical reporting.

## Responsibilities
- Manages user onboarding, account provisioning, and access permissions across all staff members.
- Configures global organizational preferences, website content (CMS), and system defaults.
- Oversees end-to-end operational workflows across all rescue, shelter, medical, adoption, foster, inventory, and financial modules.
- Monitors organizational audit logs, security events, and platform performance.
- Reviews multi-centre performance metrics, analytics, and operational reports.

## Workflow
1. Log into the PawGuard Admin Portal as Super Administrator.
2. Access the administrative menu to manage platform operations, staff accounts, or system settings.
3. Review organization-wide operational statistics across all facilities and departments.
4. Perform administrative configurations, user account updates, or role permission adjustments.
5. Provide high-level administrative approvals or resource reallocations across operational departments.
6. Hand off specific operational cases to department leads (e.g., Rescue Coordinator, Shelter Manager, Veterinarian) for execution.

## Modules / Areas Used
- User Management
- Roles & Permissions (RBAC)
- Website Management (CMS)
- Rescue Management & Dispatch
- Dog Management & Profiles
- Shelter Management
- Medical Records & Appointments
- Adoption & Foster Management
- Volunteer Management
- Inventory & Stock Control
- Finance & Donations
- Vehicle Fleet Management
- Reports & Analytics
- Audit Logs & System Settings

## Actions Available
- Register, edit, activate, deactivate, or remove user accounts.
- Configure permission rules and assign roles to organization staff.
- View, edit, and update records across all system modules.
- Publish and manage public website content, articles, success stories, and FAQs.
- Export operational, financial, and compliance reports.
- Configure global notifications and system options.

## Role Handoffs
- **To Rescue Coordinator**: Assigns incoming rescue calls or delegates operational dispatch authority.
- **To Shelter Manager**: Delegates facility capacity, kennel allocation, and daily shelter administration.
- **To Veterinarian**: Refers animals requiring specialized medical assessments or surgical procedures.
- **To Finance User**: Passes financial transaction oversight, campaign funding, and donation accounting.
