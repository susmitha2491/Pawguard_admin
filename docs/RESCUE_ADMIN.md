# Rescue Centre Admin

## Role Overview
The Rescue Centre Admin is responsible for operational oversight and facility administration at a specific PawGuard Rescue Operations Centre. This role manages local fleet vehicles, monitors rescue operations, oversees facility capacity, and tracks performance metrics for their designated centre.

## Responsibilities
- Monitors local rescue operations, incident volume, and response timelines for the assigned centre.
- Oversees assigned shelter facilities, section layouts, and overall capacity.
- Manages the centre vehicle fleet, including vehicle registration, inspection logs, and operational readiness.
- Tracks animal intake, rescue progress, and safety tag status for animals in centre care.
- Generates operational analytics and reporting for centre leadership.

## Workflow
1. Log into the PawGuard Admin Portal as Rescue Centre Admin.
2. Open the Rescue Centre Dashboard to review facility metrics, active dispatches, and fleet readiness.
3. Review vehicle availability and maintain fleet service logs.
4. Monitor active field operations and shelter occupancy for the assigned facility.
5. Provision or manage digital safety tags for rescued animals upon shelter intake.
6. Export centre performance reports to evaluate operational response times and capacity utilization.

## Modules / Areas Used
- Rescue Management & Requests
- Dispatch Tracking
- Dog Management & Profiles
- Shelter Management
- Vehicle Fleet Management
- Reports & Analytics
- Notifications

## Actions Available
- View rescue requests, assigned field agents, and dispatch timelines for the assigned facility.
- Register new rescue vehicles and update vehicle operational status (Available, Maintenance, Out of Service).
- View shelter dog profiles, rescue intake details, and lifecycle histories.
- Provision, rotate, or update digital safety tags for animals in centre care.
- Access facility-specific operational analytics and export report summaries.

## Role Handoffs
- **From Rescue Coordinator**: Receives notification of newly dispatched cases and facility arrival schedules.
- **To Rescue Agent**: Monitors field agent assignments while agents execute active field rescues.
- **To Shelter Manager**: Coordinates facility intake when field rescue teams bring animals to the shelter.
