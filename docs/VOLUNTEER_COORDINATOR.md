## 1. About the Volunteer Coordinator Role

The **Volunteer Coordinator** (`volunteer_coordinator`) is responsible for managing volunteer onboarding, volunteer applications, shift scheduling, event coordination, and attendance tracking.

### Primary Responsibilities

- **Volunteer Onboarding & Profiles**: Process volunteer registrations, review skills/interests, conduct orientation, and maintain volunteer profiles.
- **Shift Scheduling & Assignments**: Create shift rosters, assign volunteers to shelter tasks/events, and manage shift availability.
- **Attendance & Activity Tracking**: Record volunteer hours, track shift attendance, and issue volunteer service certificates.
