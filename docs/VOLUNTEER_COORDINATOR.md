# Volunteer Coordinator

## Role Overview
The Volunteer Coordinator oversees PawGuard's volunteer program. Responsible for volunteer recruitment, background screening, orientation, skill cataloging, shift scheduling, task assignments, and volunteer engagement, this role ensures reliable volunteer support across shelter care, rescue operations, community events, and administrative activities.

## Responsibilities
- Processes volunteer applications, background checks, and onboarding registrations.
- Catalogs volunteer skills, availability preferences, certifications, and interest areas (e.g., dog walking, event assistance, administrative support, transport).
- Organizes shift rosters, event support teams, and task schedules.
- Tracks volunteer hours, attendance logs, and service milestones.
- Coordinates volunteer training sessions and safety orientation.

## Workflow
1. Log into the PawGuard Admin Portal as Volunteer Coordinator.
2. Open the Volunteers module to review incoming volunteer applications and roster availability.
3. Review applicant qualifications, conduct orientation, and approve volunteer profiles.
4. Create shift rosters and assign volunteers to specific shelter duties, awareness campaigns, or rescue support tasks.
5. Record completed shift hours and update attendance logs.
6. Generate volunteer service reports and issue participation certificates.

## Modules / Areas Used
- Volunteer Directory & Management
- Schedules & Task Management
- Reports & Analytics

## Actions Available
- Register, approve, and maintain volunteer profiles, skills, and contact preferences.
- Create and publish volunteer shift schedules and event task rosters.
- Track volunteer hours served, duty check-ins, and activity logs.
- Issue volunteer service certificates and appreciation badges.
- Generate volunteer program reports and engagement metrics.

## Role Handoffs
- **To Shelter Manager / Event Leads**: Assigns trained volunteers to assist with daily shelter activities or community events.
