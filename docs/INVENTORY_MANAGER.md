# Inventory Manager

## Role Overview
The Inventory Manager maintains stock control, supply distribution, and warehouse management across all PawGuard facilities. Responsible for cataloging medical pharmaceuticals, animal food, rescue equipment, shelter sanitation supplies, and administrative assets, the Inventory Manager ensures adequate stock levels, monitors expiration dates, and coordinates purchase requisitions.

## Responsibilities
- Manages inventory catalogs, item categories, stock levels, and warehouse locations.
- Monitors stock thresholds, automated low-stock alerts, and item expiration dates.
- Processes supply requisitions from medical, shelter, and rescue dispatch teams.
- Logs inventory receipts, supplier deliveries, stock adjustments, and usage logs.
- Conducts regular inventory audits and physical stock counts.

## Workflow
1. Log into the PawGuard Admin Portal as Inventory Manager.
2. Open the Inventory module to review current stock levels, low-stock warnings, and pending supply requisitions.
3. Receive new deliveries from vendors and log items into central or facility warehouses.
4. Process supply requests from veterinary staff, shelter managers, or rescue teams and issue stock items.
5. Record stock adjustments, routine usage, or item write-offs.
6. Generate inventory valuation, consumption, and reorder reports.

## Modules / Areas Used
- Inventory & Stock Management
- Shelters & Storage Facilities
- Reports & Analytics

## Actions Available
- Add, edit, and categorize inventory items (Pharmaceuticals, Food & Nutrition, Rescue Gear, Shelter Supplies).
- Record stock entries, stock transfers between facilities, and stock deductions.
- Set minimum stock thresholds and receive automated low-stock notifications.
- Monitor item batches, lot numbers, and expiration timelines.
- Export inventory audit trails, stock reports, and purchase requisition summaries.

## Role Handoffs
- **From Veterinarian / Shelter Manager / Rescue Coordinator**: Receives supply requisitions for medicines, food, or gear.
- **To Finance User**: Submits verified purchase requisitions and supplier invoices for payment processing.
