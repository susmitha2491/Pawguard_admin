# Foster Management

## Purpose
The Foster Management module handles the temporary home placement program for rescue animals. It manages foster family registrations, evaluates home environments, coordinates placements, tracks care progress, and manages returns or permanent adoption handoffs.

## Roles Involved
- Super Administrator
- Foster Coordinator
- Shelter Manager
- Veterinarian

## Workflow
1. Prospective foster caregivers register and submit foster application details.
2. The Foster Coordinator reviews applicant home environments, household preferences, and experience levels.
3. Rescue animals requiring temporary home care (e.g., medical recovery, socialization, young puppies) are identified.
4. The Foster Coordinator matches eligible animals with approved foster families and executes placement agreements.
5. Food, medical supplies, and care kits are issued to the foster family.
6. The coordinator tracks active placements through regular progress check-ins and medical appointment scheduling.
7. Upon placement conclusion, the animal returns to the shelter or transitions to permanent adoption.

## Main Operations
- Register, screen, and maintain foster caregiver profiles, preferences, and availability status.
- Match rescue animals with compatible foster homes based on care needs.
- Issue and track foster placement agreements, care kits, and supply disbursements.
- Log foster progress reports, weight trends, and behavioral observations.
- Monitor active placement durations and schedule follow-up check-ins.

## Status / Lifecycle
A foster placement progresses through the following stages:

Application Submitted → Approved Caregiver → Matched & Placed → Active Care → Placement Concluded

- **Application Submitted**: Caregiver registration received.
- **Approved Caregiver**: Caregiver background and home environment verified.
- **Matched & Placed**: Pet selected and transferred to foster home with agreement.
- **Active Care**: Ongoing home care monitored with regular updates.
- **Placement Concluded**: Pet returned to shelter or finalized for permanent adoption.

## Role Handoffs
- **Veterinarian / Shelter Manager → Foster Coordinator**: Animal identified as needing temporary home care.
- **Foster Coordinator → Foster Caregiver**: Pet and supply kit delivered for active care.
- **Foster Coordinator → Adoption Coordinator**: Animal prepared for permanent adoption listing upon care completion.

## Related Process
Connects with **Dog Management** for placement status tracking, **Veterinary & Medical** for medical follow-ups, and **Inventory Management** for foster supply kits.
