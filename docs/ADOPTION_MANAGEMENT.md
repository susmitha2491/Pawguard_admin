# Adoption Management

## Purpose
The Adoption Management module governs the end-to-end pet adoption workflow. It manages public adoption listings, processes applicant submissions, logs applicant screening and home evaluations, issues adoption contracts, and tracks post-adoption follow-ups.

## Roles Involved
- Super Administrator
- Adoption Coordinator
- Shelter Manager
- Veterinarian

## Workflow
1. Animals receiving medical clearance and behavioral approval are published on the public adoption portal.
2. Prospective adopters browse adoptable pet profiles and submit online adoption applications.
3. The Adoption Coordinator reviews applications, conducts phone interviews, and logs applicant evaluation scores.
4. Home environment assessments or virtual home visits are scheduled and recorded.
5. Meet-and-greet sessions between the applicant and pet are organized at the shelter.
6. Upon application approval, the adoption agreement and fee receipt are processed.
7. Ownership is transferred, digital safety tags are re-registered to the adopter, and post-adoption check-ins are scheduled.

## Main Operations
- Publish, edit, and maintain public adoptable pet profiles with photos, traits, and compatibility notes.
- Process incoming adoption applications and manage applicant communication.
- Log screening evaluations, interview notes, and home visit assessments.
- Generate adoption agreements, fee receipts, and digital ownership transfer documents.
- Track adoption metrics, placement conversion rates, and post-adoption follow-up milestones.

## Status / Lifecycle
An adoption application moves through the following stages:

Submitted → Under Review → Interview & Screening → Approved → Adoption Finalized

- **Submitted**: New adoption application received from prospective owner.
- **Under Review**: Coordinator evaluating applicant qualifications and pet suitability.
- **Interview & Screening**: Applicant interview and home evaluation in progress.
- **Approved**: Applicant verified and approved for adoption.
- **Adoption Finalized**: Contract signed, fee processed, pet handed over, and profile updated to Adopted.

## Role Handoffs
- **Veterinarian & Shelter Manager → Adoption Coordinator**: Medically cleared pet published for adoption.
- **Adoption Coordinator → Adopter**: Pet, digital safety tag, Pet Passport, and adoption agreement delivered to new owner.

## Related Process
Integrates with **Dog Management** for status updates, **QR Safety Tag System** for owner re-registration, and **Finance & Donations** for adoption fee processing.
