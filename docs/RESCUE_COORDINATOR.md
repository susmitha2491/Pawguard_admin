# Rescue Coordinator

## Role Overview
The Rescue Coordinator manages the intake, verification, triage, and team dispatch for emergency animal rescue requests. Acting as the operational hub between reporting citizens and field agents, the Rescue Coordinator ensures rapid response, appropriate resource allocation, and continuous lifecycle tracking of every rescue incident.

## Responsibilities
- Receives and logs incoming emergency rescue calls from public channels or field reports.
- Verifies incident details, assesses severity, and determines emergency urgency.
- Selects and assigns qualified Rescue Agents and fleet vehicles for field dispatch.
- Monitors active rescues in real-time through the chronological lifecycle tracker (Reported → Verified → Dispatched → En Route → Located → Secured → Admitted).
- Communicates updates to field rescue teams and coordinates facility reception with shelter managers.

## Workflow
1. Log into the PawGuard Admin Portal as Rescue Coordinator.
2. Review new incoming rescue reports on the Rescue Coordinator Dashboard.
3. Verify incident location, animal physical condition, severity level, and reporter notes.
4. Open the Dispatch modal to select an available Rescue Agent and an operational fleet vehicle.
5. Confirm the assignment to transition the case to Dispatched status and notify the assigned agent.
6. Track the live progress as the field agent moves through En Route, Located, Secured, and Shelter Admission stages.
7. Confirm successful transfer to the shelter facility to conclude the dispatch phase.

## Modules / Areas Used
- Rescue Requests
- Rescue Dispatch
- Dog Management
- Shelter Directory
- Notifications

## Actions Available
- Log emergency rescue reports with reporter contact details, physical condition notes, and location map pinpoints.
- Verify rescue reports and set priority levels (Low, Medium, High, Critical) or mark emergency urgency.
- Reject or close invalid or duplicate reports with documented rationale.
- Assign Rescue Coordinators, Rescue Agents, drivers, vehicles, and specialized equipment to cases.
- Monitor real-time lifecycle progress stepper from initial report through shelter admission.

## Role Handoffs
- **From Reporter / Citizen**: Receives initial incident reports and distress calls.
- **To Rescue Agent**: Dispatches assigned agent and vehicle to travel to the incident location.
- **To Shelter Manager**: Alerts shelter staff of incoming rescued animals requiring intake and medical evaluation.
