# Veterinary & Medical

## Purpose
The Veterinary & Medical module manages clinical healthcare, diagnostic evaluations, medical treatments, surgical procedures, vaccination programs, veterinary appointments, and automated health reminders across the PawGuard organization.

## Roles Involved
- Super Administrator
- Veterinarian
- Shelter Manager
- Foster Coordinator

## Workflow
1. Newly admitted animals are scheduled for an initial medical intake examination.
2. The Veterinarian conducts the exam, logs vital signs, diagnoses physical conditions, and creates a treatment plan.
3. Prescriptions, medications, vaccinations, and surgical procedures (e.g., spay/neuter) are logged in the animal's clinical record.
4. Smart automated reminders are configured for recurring booster shots, medication doses, or follow-up check-ups.
5. As treatments complete, the Veterinarian updates medical status logs and issues official health certificates.
6. Final medical clearance is issued, confirming the animal is healthy for adoption or foster care.

## Main Operations
- Create and maintain clinical medical records, examination notes, diagnostic reports, and lab results.
- Schedule and manage veterinary appointments, routine check-ups, and surgical procedures.
- Log treatments, prescription dosages, vaccination records, and deworming routines.
- Set automated smart reminders for upcoming vaccinations, medication renewals, and follow-ups.
- Generate digital health certificates, rabies vaccination documentation, and medical clearance forms.

## Status / Lifecycle
Medical records and health evaluations progress through:

Intake Exam → Under Treatment → Recovery → Medical Clearance

- **Intake Exam**: Initial health screening upon admission.
- **Under Treatment**: Receiving active medication, therapy, or post-surgical care.
- **Recovery**: Observing physical progress and convalescence.
- **Medical Clearance**: Fully healthy and approved for foster placement or adoption listing.

## Role Handoffs
- **Shelter Manager → Veterinarian**: Animal presented for intake examination or routine healthcare.
- **Veterinarian → Inventory Manager**: Pharmaceuticals, vaccines, and surgical supplies requested for treatment.
- **Veterinarian → Adoption / Foster Coordinator**: Official medical clearance certificate issued for placement.

## Related Process
Connects directly with **Dog Management** for health profile updates, **Shelter Management** for medical isolation housing, and **Certificates** for official documentation.
