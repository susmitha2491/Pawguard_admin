# Lost & Found

## Purpose
The Lost & Found module facilitates the recovery and owner reunification of lost or stray pets. It provides a public reporting system for lost pet notices and stray sightings, matches reported animals with shelter intakes, and enables digital safety tag scanning to quickly return pets to their owners.

## Roles Involved
- Super Administrator
- Rescue Coordinator
- Shelter Manager
- Adoption Coordinator
- Public / Pet Owners

## Workflow
1. A pet owner reports a missing pet, or a citizen logs a stray animal sighting on the public portal.
2. The report includes pet physical traits, photos, last known location map pins, and owner contact details.
3. The system cross-references public lost reports against newly admitted shelter animals and stray sightings.
4. When a digital safety tag is scanned or a visual match is identified, shelter staff notify the pet owner.
5. The owner verifies identity, visits the facility, and completes ownership verification.
6. The animal is reunited with the owner, and the Lost & Found ticket is closed.

## Main Operations
- Log lost pet reports and stray animal sightings with physical descriptions, landmarks, and photos.
- Search and filter public lost & found registries by location, date, breed, or color.
- Match stray sightings against lost pet listings and shelter intake records.
- Resolve public digital safety tag scans to connect finders directly with pet owners.
- Log successful pet reunifications and manage case status records.

## Status / Lifecycle
A Lost & Found report progresses through:

Reported → Matching In Progress → Potential Match → Verification → Reunification Completed

- **Reported**: Lost pet or stray sighting registered in the system.
- **Matching In Progress**: Cross-referencing against shelter intakes and public reports.
- **Potential Match**: Visual or digital safety tag match identified.
- **Verification**: Owner confirms pet identity and provides ownership proof.
- **Reunification Completed**: Pet returned to owner and ticket resolved.

## Role Handoffs
- **Public User → Rescue Coordinator / Shelter Manager**: Lost pet notice or stray sighting received for cross-matching.
- **Shelter Manager → Pet Owner**: Owner notified of pet location following positive match or tag scan.

## Related Process
Connects with **Dog Management** for pet profiles, **QR Safety Tag System** for instant scan resolution, and **Rescue Management** for stray intake handling.
