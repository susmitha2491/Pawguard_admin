# Dog Management

## Purpose
The Dog Management module provides centralized digital master profiles for all animals within the PawGuard network. It tracks identity, physical traits, health status, shelter placement, digital safety tag assignments, and care history throughout the animal's lifecycle.

## Roles Involved
- Super Administrator
- Rescue Centre Admin
- Rescue Coordinator
- Rescue Agent
- Shelter Manager
- Veterinarian
- Adoption Coordinator
- Foster Coordinator

## Workflow
1. A new animal profile is created automatically during rescue intake or manually by shelter staff.
2. Shelter staff record physical details, estimated age, breed, gender, color, behavioral traits, and photo evidence.
3. The veterinary team links medical records, vaccination histories, and care requirements to the profile.
4. Shelter managers assign shelter locations, wards, and kennels to the animal.
5. The digital safety tag system generates a unique QR code linked to the animal's public passport.
6. The profile updates dynamically as the animal transitions through shelter care, foster placement, or adoption.

## Main Operations
- Create, edit, and search master animal profiles with photos and physical characteristics.
- Track care lifecycle status (Rescue Intake, Shelter Care, Medical Isolation, Foster Placement, Adoptable, Adopted).
- Provision, rotate, or deactivate digital safety tags and QR codes.
- Access integrated medical logs, vaccination records, and shelter housing assignments.
- Generate digital Pet Passports for public identification or owner transfer.

## Status / Lifecycle
An animal's master profile moves through the following care stages:

Rescue Intake → Shelter Care → Medical Evaluation → Foster / Adoptable → Adopted

- **Rescue Intake**: Newly admitted animal from field rescue operations.
- **Shelter Care**: Resident in shelter receiving daily care and housing.
- **Medical Evaluation**: Under active veterinary exam, treatment, or recovery.
- **Foster / Adoptable**: Cleared for temporary foster placement or public adoption listing.
- **Adopted**: Permanently placed with an approved adopter.

## Role Handoffs
- **Rescue Agent → Shelter Manager**: Animal master profile established at shelter admission.
- **Shelter Manager → Veterinarian**: Animal profile referenced for health check-ups and medical treatments.
- **Veterinarian → Adoption / Foster Coordinator**: Profile updated with medical clearance for placement.

## Related Process
Connects directly with **Shelter Management** for housing, **Veterinary & Medical** for clinical care, **QR Safety Tag System** for identification, and **Adoption / Foster Management** for placements.
