# Finance & Donations

## Purpose
The Finance & Donations module manages financial accounting, public and corporate contributions, fundraising campaigns, operational expenses, and fiscal reporting within the PawGuard network.

## Roles Involved
- Super Administrator
- Finance User
- Adoption Coordinator

## Workflow
1. Public donations, online contributions, corporate sponsorships, and adoption fees are recorded in the central financial ledger.
2. Funds are allocated to general operations, medical emergency funds, shelter maintenance, or specific rescue campaigns.
3. Automated tax-deductible donation receipts and contribution acknowledgments are issued to donors.
4. Department expense claims, vendor invoices, and purchase orders are submitted for payment.
5. The Finance User verifies claims, approves disbursements, and logs expenditure receipts.
6. Monthly financial audits, balance sheets, and campaign performance reports are generated for executive review.

## Main Operations
- Record online and offline donations, donor details, payment channels, and target fund allocations.
- Manage fundraising campaigns, financial targets, and campaign progress metrics.
- Process operational expenditures, utility bills, fleet fuel claims, and supplier invoices.
- Issue digital receipts and tax documentation to contributors.
- Generate financial audit trails, revenue vs expense charts, and budget reports.

## Status / Lifecycle
Financial transactions move through:

Transaction Logged → Verification → Ledger Allocation → Receipt Issued

- **Transaction Logged**: New donation or expense entry created.
- **Verification**: Transaction details and payment confirmation verified.
- **Ledger Allocation**: Amount assigned to specific operational fund or campaign.
- **Receipt Issued**: Official documentation generated and delivered to donor or vendor.

## Role Handoffs
- **Inventory Manager → Finance User**: Supplier invoices and purchase requisitions submitted for disbursement.
- **Finance User → Super Administrator**: Financial audit reports provided for organization-wide governance.

## Related Process
Connects with **Adoption Management** for fee processing, **Inventory Management** for purchase orders, and **Reports & Analytics** for fiscal auditing.
